Will dual-camera recording work on my phone?

Dual-camera operation depends on your device’s capabilities. Check the app listing for your platform and confirm that your device supports the recording mode you want to use.

Where are my recordings stored?

Recordings and trip data stay on your device unless you choose to share them. Read the privacy policy for details.

Can I share an important clip?

Yes. Use the app’s sharing controls to send important recordings to an insurance agent, law enforcement, or a trusted contact when needed.

What should I know about longer drives?

Dual-camera recording, optional audio, GPS, and trip overlays can increase device temperature. Keep your phone out of direct sunlight when possible and consider directing an air-conditioning vent toward it during long drives.

Which permissions does Guardian use?

Review our permissions page for details about camera, microphone, location, and other app permissions.
