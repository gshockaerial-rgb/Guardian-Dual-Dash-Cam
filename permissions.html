Guardian Dual Dash Cam

Required app permissions

Guardian only requests access needed for recording, trip context, impact detection, storage, and sharing.

Camera

Used to record the road and cabin when supported by your device.

Microphone

Used only when optional audio recording is enabled.

Location

Used to add route, speed, street, date, and time context.

Motion sensors

Used for impact detection and sudden-bump recognition.

Photos and storage

Used to save, organize, play, lock, delete, and share recordings.

Notifications and background use

May show recording status and support recording-related functions while driving.
